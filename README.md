# In Countless Shadows

*An experimental poetry game built around Shakespeare's Sonnet 17.*

In Sonnet 17, Shakespeare imagines his words surviving into a future that no longer believes them. His verse might preserve the person he loves, or become a tomb for someone readers can no longer recognize.

*In Countless Shadows* makes this tension playable. By rearranging language and following relationships of rhyme, rhythm, and argument, readers explore how much a poem's form can do, and where any set of rules stops short of interpretation.

The project is both an artwork and an inquiry into what happens when reading becomes something we can act on. It is being developed in Godot.
